      How to Play

      Objective

      Fill every white cell with letters by solving the clues provided for each word. Dark cells separate words.

      Navigation & Selection

      Tap any cell to focus it. Tap the highlighted cell a second time or press the DIR button to toggle between Across and Down.

      Typing & Correction

      Type letters on your hardware keyboard or tap the touch keys. The cursor moves to the next square automatically. Press Backspace to erase.

      Daily Puzzles & Tiers

      Every day at midnight UK time, a fresh puzzle releases in Mini, Midi, and Main grid sizes. Revisit previous puzzles anytime via the Vault.
